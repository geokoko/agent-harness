# Product context and continuity

Use this when beginning a product engagement or when existing context
conflicts. Read the smallest relevant sources first. Follow the
repository's instructions and reuse the current product, brand, roadmap
and experiment documents. Preserve their ownership and format; do not
create a second source of truth.

If no suitable record exists and the request authorizes creating
artifacts, use `docs/product/brief.md` and `docs/product/experiments.md`
in the product repository, or a workspace the user supplies for a
product without one, and disclose the chosen paths. A conversation-only
consultation needs neither file. If those destinations are not writable,
return the proposed content without claiming it was saved. Customer
information does not belong in the skill registry.

## Compact brief

Record established facts and consequential unknowns, not a fictional
persona or an exhaustive business plan. Link to an existing source
instead of duplicating it. This is a checklist to adapt, not a list of
questions to put to the user one by one.

| Field | Useful content |
|---|---|
| Product and stage | Name, URL or repository, current availability, source date |
| Customer | Buyer, user, beneficiary, problem, trigger, current workaround |
| Value and proof | Intended outcome, differentiation, verified claims and limits |
| Offer | Current or proposed price, packaging, commitment, open decisions |
| Adoption | Discovery, first-value event, onboarding friction, repeat-use reason |
| Evidence | Customer observations, analytics, research, source links, limitations |
| Current bet | Desired outcome, selected bottleneck, next move, what is deferred |
| Constraints | Time, money, team, geography, brand, readiness, available tools |
| Execution authority | Approved actions, accounts, audiences, content bounds, limits |
| Open questions | Material unknown, owner, smallest useful evidence to obtain |

Label proposed positioning and prices as proposals. A historic approved
plan does not establish current delivery. Compare implementation,
deployment, customer evidence and explicit founder decisions at the
level the current task needs. Record unresolved contradictions and keep
them out of public claims. Absent analytics do not mean zero users or
zero demand.

## One experiment record

Keep each experiment small enough that its result can inform a decision:

```text
Experiment / date / owner:
Question and hypothesis:
Audience and scope:
What changes; what stays comparable:
Offer/message and actual asset links:
Channel, account, authorization, and budget/time limits:
Baseline or missing baseline; metric, denominator, cohort, time window:
Measurement source and attribution limitations:
Target and stop/continue rule:
Execution state and verification links:
Observed result (or not yet measured):
Interpretation, uncertainty, and next decision:
```

Record failed attempts and limits as well as successes. Preserve past
results: append the new decision rather than rewriting history to fit a
conclusion. Keep detailed measurements in their owning source and
summarize only the values needed to decide. Refresh the affected facts
when the product, audience, offer or measurement method changes, and
retain evidence whose applicability is established.
