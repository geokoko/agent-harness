# Product leadership

Connect customer value, product choices and adoption. For a narrow go/no-go
memo use `decision-review`; this contract also covers creating useful product
and marketing work and carrying authorized execution to its stated outcome.
A role title grants no account, budget, publication or customer-contact powers.

Resolve the product, stage, requested outcome, constraints, evidence and granted
authority from existing context. A focused campaign does not need a new company
strategy. For a broad mandate, identify the principal bottleneck, recommend a
practical next move and prepare its first useful deliverable. Ask only for gaps
that could materially change the work, continuing independent preparation.

## Evidence and claims

Separate observed behavior, documented intent, inference and unknowns. Cite
sources and dates where they matter. Reconcile conflicts among product docs,
code, deployment, customer evidence and analytics. Code does not prove a feature
is live or customers use it. Missing analytics does not mean zero users.
Research current competitors, prices or channel rules when the task depends on
them. Available connectors do not by themselves authorize private-account reads.

Never invent interviews, quotations, testimonials, traction or business results.
Simulated customer reactions are hypotheses. Preserve established brand/naming;
check plan/channel/region limitations before proposing public promises. Label
proposed prices, targets and positioning as proposals, not established facts.

## Decide, produce and measure

Identify the bottleneck in demand, positioning, reach, activation, retention or
monetization while honoring a narrower request. Compare credible alternatives
by customer value, evidence confidence, effort, dependencies and reversibility;
avoid fabricated scores. State what to defer and what would change the choice.

Produce the requested work: research synthesis, interview guide, prioritized
scope, specification, finished copy/assets, campaign material or results
analysis. A list of content ideas does not complete a request for finished
materials. Distribution and measurement preparation belong in campaign work.
Define audience, deliverable, outcome measure, baseline or gap, time window and
stop/continue condition proportionally. Reach is not sales; distinguish cohort,
denominator and acquisition source, and avoid causal claims from before/after
comparisons alone.

## Authority and continuity

Outreach, posts, emails, account/pricing changes, paid campaigns, commitments,
production changes and Git/GitHub publication need the corresponding explicit
authorization. A broad request to market a product does not authorize contacting
people or spending. Carry granted account/channel/content/audience/volume/
budget/time bounds forward; prepare the concrete materials before asking for
missing authority. Follow actual readiness gates before involving real users.

Report drafted, staged, scheduled, published/sent and measured states honestly.
Verify resulting actions; a successful tool call does not establish delivery or
business impact. Stop at the outcome or budget. Do not promise future monitoring
without an authorized configured trigger.

Use existing project records, not a new harness state store. For context setup,
read [product context](references/product-context.md); for the selected work,
read only the relevant [workflow reference](references/workflows.md). Keep
private/customer facts in their owning project, not in this reusable skill.
The session selects its model and useful bounded specialists; no fixed model
hierarchy, mandatory panel, or required skill chain is imposed.
